# Business Context

## 1. Company Scenario

SnackFlow Foods is a fictional food manufacturing company producing packaged potato chips for retail customers.

Its production process depends on the timely availability of raw materials and packaging components such as:

- potatoes;
- vegetable oil;
- salt;
- flavourings;
- packaging bags;
- labels;
- cardboard boxes.

The case study focuses on the coordination between Purchasing, Inventory Management, Production Planning and Quality Management.

---

## 2. Current Business Situation

The coordination between Production and Purchasing still relies partly on fragmented and manual processes.

Operational information is exchanged using:

- Excel spreadsheets;
- emails;
- manual stock checks;
- urgent purchase requests;
- disconnected production and purchasing information.

Production requirements are therefore not always immediately reflected in procurement decisions.

Purchasing teams may discover material shortages only shortly before the planned production date.

---

## 3. Business Problems

This fragmented operating model creates several business problems:

- limited visibility of future material requirements;
- late identification of shortages;
- emergency purchasing;
- production delays;
- duplicated data entry;
- inconsistent information between departments;
- weak traceability of procurement and production decisions;
- excessive dependence on manual controls;
- reactive rather than preventive decision-making.

---

## 4. Business Impact

These problems may have direct operational and financial consequences.

Potential impacts include:

- higher purchasing costs due to emergency orders;
- production interruptions;
- delayed customer deliveries;
- inefficient inventory levels;
- additional administrative workload;
- reduced ability to anticipate supply risks;
- difficulty identifying the origin of operational problems.

---

## 5. Target Business Process

SnackFlow Foods wants to explore a more integrated planning approach inspired by SAP S/4HANA.

The target process should connect:

1. production requirements;
2. material requirements;
3. current inventory;
4. procurement proposals;
5. supplier information;
6. quality inspection results;
7. production readiness.

The objective is to move from fragmented manual coordination toward a more integrated and data-driven business process.

---

## 6. SAP Functional Scope

The case study focuses on four functional concepts.

### MM — Materials Management

Supports material master data, purchasing, suppliers, inventory and procurement activities.

### PP — Production Planning

Supports production planning and the calculation of manufacturing requirements.

### MRP — Material Requirements Planning

Compares production requirements with available inventory and identifies materials that need to be procured or replenished.

### QM — Quality Management

Ensures that received materials can be inspected and that quality status is considered before materials are released for production.

---

## 7. Business Objective

The objective of this project is to develop a functional simulation demonstrating how an integrated MM/PP/MRP/QM approach could help SnackFlow Foods:

- anticipate material requirements;
- identify potential shortages before production;
- generate procurement proposals;
- integrate quality controls into material availability;
- improve traceability between purchasing and production;
- reduce emergency purchasing;
- improve production readiness;
- provide operational and management KPIs.

---

## 8. Expected Business Value

The proposed approach should demonstrate how better integration between procurement, inventory, quality and production planning can support:

- proactive decision-making;
- improved material availability;
- fewer production disruptions;
- better procurement planning;
- stronger process traceability;
- improved collaboration between Purchasing and Production;
- better visibility for operational management.
